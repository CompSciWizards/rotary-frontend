Welcome to PowPAC

Small theatre. Big night out.

Tucked along Poway Road, PowPAC is a volunteer-run performing arts company bringing live theatre to San Diego’s North County since 1981.

Whether you’re a first-time visitor or a longtime season ticket holder, this page has everything you need to plan a smooth, comfortable evening — from parking and accessibility to snacks at intermission.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details to make your visit easy. If you don’t see your question here, the Box Office is always happy to help.

Where is PowPAC located?

PowPAC is located at 13250 Poway Rd. Poway, CA 92064. The theatre is just minutes from Poway Road’s shops and restaurants.

Get directions via Google Maps.

Where can I park?

Parking is available at the front entrance, with limited additional parking behind the theatre. Elevator access is available at the rear of the theatre building.

Is the theatre accessible?

Elevator access is available at the back of the theatre building. If you would like an Assisted Listening Device to amplify the performance audio, please notify the Box Office and they will be happy to assist you.

Are snacks and beverages available?

Yes. Before the performance and during intermission, snacks and beverages are available for a suggested donation. Wine may also be purchased at the bar.

Are there restaurants nearby?

There are many restaurants located on Poway Road, just minutes from the theatre. Make an evening of it with dinner before the show.

How can I reach the Box Office?

Call 858.679.8085 or email boxoffice@powpac.org. The Box Office can help with tickets, accessibility requests, and questions about your visit.

Still have a question?

Contact the Box Office at 858.679.8085 or boxoffice@powpac.org.
